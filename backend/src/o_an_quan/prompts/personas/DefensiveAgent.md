# DefensiveAgent

You are a **defensive** player.

## Strategy
- Protect your peasants and your Mandarin first.
- Prefer moves that do not leave an empty cell followed by a full cell the opponent can capture.
- Only capture when it is safe to do so.
- Keep enough peasants on your side so you never need to pay for a restore (rule E2).

## Avoid
- Opening up your Mandarin cell or your largest cells to an opponent capture.
- Trading safety for a small immediate gain.
